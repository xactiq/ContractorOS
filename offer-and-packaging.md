# ContractorOS Offer and Packaging

## Product name
**ContractorOS**

## One-line pitch
A white-label contractor CRM + estimating + supplement + job-doc system that helps small contractors close faster, supplement faster, and run tighter.

## Who it is for
- roofing companies
- restoration contractors
- small general contractors
- insurance-focused exterior contractors
- supplement-heavy operators who are still running on messy spreadsheets

## Core promise
Give contractors one system to manage the path from lead to estimate to supplement to job completion to payment.

## What buyers are actually buying
- less chaos
- faster supplement turnaround
- cleaner paperwork
- better follow-up discipline
- better visibility into margin
- a more professional operating system without custom software pricing

## Offer structure
### Option 1: Template License
Best for DIY operators.
- core workbook/system
- branded setup guide
- default document pack
- limited support

### Option 2: Setup + Customization
Best for serious operators.
- branded buildout
- company settings and forms setup
- pricebook initialization
- workflow tuning
- onboarding session

### Option 3: Managed Optimization
Best for owners who want recurring help.
- quarterly updates
- document revisions
- pricing refresh support
- workflow improvements
- KPI review / optimization

## Pricing logic
Avoid cheap pricing. This is operational leverage, not a novelty template.

Suggested starting ranges:
- Template License: $997–$2,500
- Setup + Customization: $2,500–$7,500
- Managed Optimization: monthly retainer or quarterly package

## Positioning language
### Pain-based
- Stop running your company on six broken spreadsheets.
- Stop rebuilding supplements by hand.
- Stop losing margin because your estimating, CRM, and paperwork live in different places.

### Outcome-based
- Close faster
- Supplement faster
- Run tighter
- Look more professional
- Track real profit

## Sales assets needed
- one-page product sheet
- demo workbook / sandbox brand
- short walkthrough video
- before/after workflow explanation
- sample outputs: estimate, supplement, contract, invoice, dashboard

## Strongest wedge
Lead with supplement speed + operational clarity.

Why:
- contractors hate admin drag
- supplement turnaround ties directly to cash and credibility
- CRM alone feels generic
- estimating alone feels commodity
- the combined system feels valuable

## White-label promise
This should be designed so a buyer can swap:
- logo
- company name
- colors
- legal text
- payment terms
- contact info
- document footer/header

without breaking the core engine.

## What not to sell it as
- not “just a spreadsheet”
- not “AI roofing software” hype nonsense
- not “fully automated supplements” with zero review

## Better framing
- contractor operating system
- restoration estimating and supplement system
- claims workflow operating system
- white-label contractor admin and estimating platform
